## Modes

- **Hardware:** an insert effect with subtle hum, resistive loss, scratch or an occasional fault.
- **Play:** MIDI, GUI gestures, automation or the 1 to 64 step Gesture Lane disturb the hardware in time.
- **Create:** internal excitation makes one-shots and loops on a silent track and renders them to WAV.

Every fault has a cause (movement, wear, pressure, touch, mains hum, or an excitation you switch on), so a still,
shielded, healthy circuit is silent. The Environment (mains hum, dimmer buzz, radio interference, ground loop,
nearby phone) is shared by both slots.
